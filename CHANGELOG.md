## Unreleased

- The site: a static generator in Ruby over the census's records. One page per shape with a plain-WebGL viewer that draws the shape, its tiling chunk, or its corona witness from cells inlined in the page, the verdict in plain words, the stages, downloads through jsDelivr, and the record as stored with syntax highlighting. An index per size, paginated at 100. Tag pages for non-tilers, open, Heesch 1 and 2, box, torus, chiral, achiral, and corona. Home with the scoreboard and the findings. Prose pages from markdown: glossary, verify, thanks, references, about. Nicknames from `content/names.yml`. Light and dark, following the system.
