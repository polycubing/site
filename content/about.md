The Polycube Tiling Census is by [Shane Becker](https://veganstraightedge.com). It began in July 2026 with a question the literature did not answer: what is the smallest polycube that cannot tile space? The answer, the 3×3 square ring, took eight days. Finishing every nonacube took the rest of the summer.

Everything is public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The data and code live at [github.com/polycubing/census](https://github.com/polycubing/census). This site is generated from that data, and [its own code](https://github.com/polycubing/site) is public too.

Shape ids are permanent. `9/2127` will always be the staircase, here and in any paper that cites it.
