Prior work the census builds on, and the places it diverges from.

- Craig S. Kaplan, [*Heesch Numbers of Unmarked Polyforms*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.09438), 2021. The two-dimensional census and the method this one lifts into three dimensions.
- Joseph Myers, [*Polyform tiling*](https://www.polyomino.org.uk/mathematics/polyform-tiling/). The software and data behind the two-dimensional tiling censuses, 2000 to 2024.
- Craig S. Kaplan, [*Detecting Isohedral Polyforms with a SAT Solver*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16407), 2024.
- Brakensiek, Heule, Mackey, Narváez, *The Resolution of Keller's Conjecture*, 2020. A cube-tiling problem settled by reduction to SAT, and the closest methodological kin to this census.
- Siddhartha Bhattacharya, *Periodicity and decidability of tilings of Z²*, American Journal of Mathematics 142, 2020. Why the two-dimensional question is decidable, and why the three-dimensional one is open.
- Rachel Greenfeld and Terence Tao, *A counterexample to the periodic tiling conjecture*, Annals of Mathematics 200, 2024.
- Dress, Huson, Molnár, on the classification of tile-transitive tilings in three dimensions, which is infinite, and the reason no three-dimensional analogue of the two-dimensional method exists.
- Bojan Bašić, *A figure with Heesch number 6*, The Mathematical Intelligencer 43, 2021. The standing two-dimensional record.
- Marijn Heule, [drat-trim](https://github.com/marijnheule/drat-trim). The proof checker.
- Armin Biere, [kissat](https://github.com/arminbiere/kissat) and [CaDiCaL](https://github.com/arminbiere/cadical). The solvers.
- [OEIS A000162](https://oeis.org/A000162) and [A038119](https://oeis.org/A038119). The shape counts the census must reproduce before anything else runs.
